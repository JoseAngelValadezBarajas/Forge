## Commercial

Forge Commercial is a separate license granted by express written agreement from the holder of the applicable rights. A commercial agreement may authorize uses that are not permitted by the Community terms, subject to the scope and conditions of that agreement.

Commercial authorization is required before using Forge for a commercial purpose outside the rights granted by PolyForm. In particular, a commercial business must obtain authorization before internal use for a commercial purpose, unless the specific use is independently a permitted purpose under PolyForm. This includes when applicable:

- internal use by a commercial business;
- commercial hosted, managed or SaaS services;
- OEM products or embedded distributions;
- commercial redistribution, resale or bundling; and
- commercial integrations or offerings based on Forge.

## Request process

1. Describe the organization, product, deployment model and intended use.
2. Identify whether the request involves internal use, hosted services, OEM, redistribution, resale or another commercial use.
3. Identify the Forge revision and any modifications or integrations.
4. Obtain a written agreement signed or otherwise expressly accepted by the authorized rights holder before beginning the proposed use.

The public repository does not itself create a commercial offer, commercial authorization, price, service commitment or reusable commercial contract. A GitHub issue, discussion, pull request, public comment or informal conversation is not a commercial agreement.
